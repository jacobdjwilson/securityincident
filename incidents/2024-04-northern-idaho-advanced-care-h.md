## Incident Overview

Northern Idaho Advanced Care Hospital, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Northern Idaho Advanced Care Hospital affecting 2,728 residents.
