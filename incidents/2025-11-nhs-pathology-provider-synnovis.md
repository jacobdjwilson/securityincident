## Incident Overview

NHS Pathology Provider Synnovis, operating within the Healthcare sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident classified as Protected Health Information (PHI) Breach. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Healthcare data breach disclosure submitted to federal regulators by NHS Pathology Provider Synnovis.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

The specific volume of affected customer or organizational records remains under active forensic investigation. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `nhspathologyprovidersynnovis.com`.

Identified categories of compromised data and impacted assets include:
- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Patient Medical Records
