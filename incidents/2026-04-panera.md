## Incident Overview

Panera, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
