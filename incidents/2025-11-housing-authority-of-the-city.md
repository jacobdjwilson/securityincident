## Incident Overview

Housing Authority of the City of Vancouver, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Housing Authority of the City of Vancouver affecting 736 residents.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 736 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `housingauthorityofthecity.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
