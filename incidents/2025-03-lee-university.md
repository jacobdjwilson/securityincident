## Incident Overview

Lee University, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 1,167 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `leeuniversity.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
