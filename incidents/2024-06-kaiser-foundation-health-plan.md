## Incident Overview

Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Kaiser Foundation Health Plan affecting 17,530 residents.

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 17,530 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `kaiserfoundationhealthplan.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
