## Incident Overview

Campus CE, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Campus CE affecting 52,175 residents.

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 52,175 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `campusce.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
