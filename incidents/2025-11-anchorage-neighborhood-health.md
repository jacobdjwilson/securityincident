## Incident Overview

Anchorage Neighborhood Health Center, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Anchorage Neighborhood Health Center affecting 3,226 residents.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 3,226 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `anchorageneighborhoodhealth.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
