## Incident Overview

Alera Group, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Alera Group affecting 27,901 residents.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 27,901 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `aleragroup.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
