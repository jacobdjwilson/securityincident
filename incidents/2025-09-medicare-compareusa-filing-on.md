## Incident Overview

Medicare CompareUSA filing on behalf of Aetna, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Medicare CompareUSA filing on behalf of Aetna affecting 808 residents.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 808 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `medicarecompareusafilingon.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
