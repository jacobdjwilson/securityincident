## Incident Overview

Adapt Health, within its operating sector, has been subject to a cybersecurity incident. Ground-truth telemetry indicates the threat activity is currently unconfirmed or under forensic attribution.

Washington State Attorney General formal data breach disclosure notice filed by Adapt Health affecting 1,200 residents.

## Compromised Assets & Data Scope

Current disclosures quantify the affected population at approximately 1,200 records/individuals. Primary affected online infrastructure and perimeter domains include `adapthealth.com`.

Specific classes of compromised records, credentials, or proprietary information continue to be audited through ongoing forensic investigation.
